## Step 1: How to measure an uneven UK alcove

Never take just one width measurement and assume the whole recess is the same. Measure every shelf height individually:

1. **Measure at the exact height of each shelf:** Take three width readings at each planned shelf level — against the back wall, halfway forward, and at the front edge of the chimney breast.
2. **Work off the tightest width — or plan a deliberate shadow gap:**
   - **Floating look with a shadow gap (easiest and cleanest):** Order shelves **3cm to 5cm shorter** than the narrowest width on each side (6cm to 10cm shorter in total). A deliberate shadow gap hides crooked plaster completely and makes fitting pre-drilled hidden fixings far easier.
   - **Wall-to-wall tight fit:** If you want wall-to-wall shelves inside an out-of-square recess, allow a couple of millimetres clearance or expect to scribe the ends carefully on site.
3. **Check depth against the chimney breast projection:** Measure how far the chimney breast sticks out into the room, then choose a shelf depth that sits cleanly inside it without overhanging awkwardly into the walkway.

## Step 3: Vertical spacing rules for alcove shelves

Good vertical spacing stops alcoves looking either cramped or sparse:

- **Above a base cupboard or sideboard:** Leave **35cm to 45cm** between the top of the cupboard and the underside of the first shelf so lamps, frames or trailing plants have breathing room.
- **Between shelves:** **30cm to 35cm** clear vertical gap works for most UK living rooms (tall enough for hardbacks and framed prints without looking gappy). If you are storing 12-inch vinyl records or tall ring binders, allow **35cm to 38cm**.
- **Top shelf to ceiling:** Leave roughly **35cm to 45cm** below the ceiling or coving so the room does not feel top-heavy.
- **Aligning with your oak mantel beam:** A clean designer trick is lining up the middle alcove shelf level with the top surface of your [solid oak mantel beam](https://www.craftwoodfurniture.co.uk/products/solid-oak-mantel-beam) so the eye reads one calm horizontal line across the whole chimney wall.

## Questions people ask

### How far apart should alcove floating shelves be spaced?

For most UK living room alcoves, **30cm to 35cm** between shelves gives enough height for hardbacks and decor without leaving awkward gaps, with **35cm to 45cm** above any lower cupboard or sideboard.

### Should alcove shelves go wall-to-wall or leave a gap?

Leaving a small shadow gap of 3cm to 5cm at each end is usually cleaner in older UK houses because chimney breast plaster is rarely square, and it makes sliding a floating shelf onto concealed rods much easier.
